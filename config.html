Sticks

Roles
Whichever stick is not pointing does the scrolling. Both point gives you a coarse thumb and a fine one.
Fine stick-
Second stick speed, as a share of the first. Only used when both point.

Speed

Pointer-
Pixels per second at full push. Reads high because macOS scales raw movement to about a third on the way to the cursor.

Speed dial

Trigger
The triggers are analogue, so this slides speed continuously as you squeeze rather than switching at a threshold. A trigger used this way stops clicking, and its click moves to the bumper above it.
At full squeeze-
Speed at the bottom of the trigger travel. Under 100 slows down for precision, over 100 speeds up.

Gyro

Mode
Any angle finds gravity with the accelerometer and measures turning about it, so how you hold the controller stops mattering. Use it if the controller is on its end, tipped forward, or moving with you. Fixed grip is cheaper but only correct for the grip you declare below.
Grip
Only read in fixed grip mode. On end is the one-handed hold, pointing away from you like a remote.
When live
Assign the gyro action to a control to hold or toggle it. Always on will wander while you walk, since every step is a rotation. Hold is the equivalent of lifting a mouse.
Sensitivity-
Pixels per degree of rotation. A wrist sweep is around 40 degrees.
Flip left and right
Flip up and down
Sign conventions vary by how the controller is turned. If it goes the wrong way, flip it here rather than re-gripping.

The resting reading is learned continuously, so the cursor does not slide away on its own. That calibration keeps running even while the gyro is gated off, so it is already settled the moment you enable it.

What the machine sees

Declare a mouse and nothing else -
Off, the dongle also declares a keyboard and a gamepad it never uses. macOS finds the unused keyboard, cannot identify it, and opens Keyboard Setup Assistant asking for the key beside left Shift, which does not exist. On, there is one pointer and this serial port. Identity is fixed when the device is plugged in, so this reboots the dongle.

More than one controller

When two are connected -
Separate keeps each controller on its own player slot, so only the first one drives the pointer. Act as one folds them together: buttons from either count as pressed, and each stick axis takes whichever controller is pushed furthest from centre. That is how a pair of Joy-Cons becomes a single controller with two sticks — the left one holds the left stick, the right one the right, and neither overwrites the other. Stored in flash and read when the router starts, so this reboots the dongle.

Up to four controllers can be connected at once.
